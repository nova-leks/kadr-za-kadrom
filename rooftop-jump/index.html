Step 3

Open Seedance 2.5

  1. Sign in (1 Telegram, 2 Google, 3 Email) and open Seedance on syntx.ai.
  2. The top of the screen should say Seedance – 2.5 A. If it shows another version, tap that line and choose 2.5.
  3. The form is at the bottom of the screen: the prompt field C, the paperclip for files B, the settings icon D and the generation price in tokens E.

Screenshots are from the Russian interface. The buttons are in the same places in English.

If something looks different

A red asterisk next to the model name and a hint like “Attach at least one video”? That's normal until you upload the source video (step 5).

Not enough tokens? The generation price is shown next to the send button (E). When I checked on October 14, 2026, it was about 173 tokens in Edit mode, and a new free account had 5.5. You can top up from the profile menu, in the Plans section. Prices and terms change, so check the current ones.

Step 4

Set the parameters

  1. Tap the settings icon D next to “720p”.
  2. In the Mode field choose Edit F. This mode swaps the characters in the uploaded video. Until a video is uploaded, you'll see a red hint under the field.
  3. The Resolution field G is set to 720p. For better quality choose 1080p (the list has 480p Draft, 720p and 1080p) and check the price after you choose.
  4. The aspect ratio must be 9:16, like the source video. In Edit mode the field shows “Auto” and can't be changed. Duration H is “Auto” too.
  5. Leave the Audio switch I on and tap Confirm.
Step 5

Upload the video and the two characters

  1. Tap the paperclip B and choose Upload file.
  2. Upload the files in this order:
1 · 03_Source_Video.mp4the whole source video, don't trim it
2 · 01_Image_1_Man.pngImage 1: the main character who runs and jumps
3 · 02_Image_2_Woman.pngImage 2: the character in the background

Main character first, background character second. The prompt calls them Image 1 and Image 2, so the order matters. The upload window has “Generated” and “Uploaded” tabs, with these limits: up to 30 images, 10 videos and 10 audio files.

You can use any charactersIn the example the main character is a man and the background character is a woman, but you can upload your own: a man instead of the woman, a woman instead of the man, two men, two women and so on. Photo 1 replaces the character who runs and jumps, photo 2 replaces the character in the background. If the gender is different, fix the words He/She and the height and weight in the prompt (in the MAIN CHARACTER and BACKGROUND CHARACTER blocks).
Step 6

Paste the prompt

  1. Tap the “Copy full prompt” button below.
  2. Tap the prompt field C at the bottom of the screen and paste the text (on a phone: tap and hold, then “Paste”).

Check the image referencesImage 1 is the main character, Image 2 is the background character. The prompt field has a hint: type @ to add references. If the mentions stay as plain text after pasting, delete one, type @ and pick the right uploaded file from the list.
Replace the main running and jumping man with the man from @Image 1. Replace the existing background friend with the woman from @Image 2, only where that person already appears.

Preserve the source video frame by frame: original shot order, camera movement, framing, rooftop layout, daylight, shadows, motion sequence, and timing. Limit the edit to character identity and clothing, plus removal of existing interface/text overlays.

TIMING AND AUDIO — HIGHEST PRIORITY
Keep the original action synchronized with the original soundtrack. Every running foot contact, takeoff, airborne rotation, first landing contact, hand/body contact, roll, recovery, and final reaction must occur at the same timestamp as in the source.
Use the actual uploaded source frames for the precise takeoff and first landing timestamps. Do not impose timing from another version of the clip.
Preserve the source start time and approximately 11.93-second duration. No added lead-in, trailing footage, silent ending, pauses, extra steps, extended flight, repeated actions, or new speed changes.
Keep the original audio at its original timestamps and playback speed. Do not move, stretch, regenerate, duplicate, or add sounds. Match the movement to the existing audio. Preserve any original speech, breathing, or vocal reactions, and synchronize visible mouth movements with them.
Prioritize original motion timing over any new performance or camera direction.

MAIN CHARACTER — @Image 1
Preserve the exact recognizable face, apparent age, hair, hairline, skin tone, facial hair, outfit, colors, fit, and accessories from @Image 1.
He is 175 cm tall and weighs 78 kg, with a realistic natural build. No exaggerated muscles, beautification, face drift, or outfit changes.
He follows the original protagonist’s poses, path, direction, stride timing, rotation, trajectory, landing, and recovery.

BACKGROUND CHARACTER — @Image 2
Preserve the exact recognizable face, apparent age, hair, skin tone, natural slender build, outfit, and accessories from @Image 2.
She is 174 cm tall and weighs 57 kg. At comparable distances and postures, the two characters appear nearly equal in height.
Use the existing background friend’s position, screen time, and action for the woman from @Image 2. Retain the reference’s existing reaction timing.
No new shot, cutaway, reverse angle, extra camera turn, or interruption of the main character’s action to reveal her.

OUTPUT
Retain full-screen vertical 9:16 framing. Select 1080p output where available.
Realistic handheld footage, natural skin texture, consistent daylight and motion blur.
No identity blending, duplicate people, distorted anatomy, floating, teleportation, or unexplained position changes.
Remove all existing text and interface overlays. No captions, subtitles, logos, watermarks, usernames, stickers, emojis, titles, black bars, or borders.
Keep the original action and audio aligned throughout the entire clip.

REMOVE EXISTING OVERLAYS
Remove the source video's existing social-media interface throughout every frame: the account name at the top, Follow button, AI-content label, Original audio label, and any visible interface icons or text.
Restore the underlying sky, rooftop, clothing, or scenery naturally and consistently across frames. Remove the existing overlays themselves, not just newly generated text. No blurred patches, solid boxes, replacement text, ghost lettering, or flickering remnants.
Keep the camera framing, action timestamps, clip duration, and audio timing unchanged while removing the overlays.
Step 7

Run it and download

  1. Check the price next to the send button E. If you don't have enough tokens, top up in the Plans section.
  2. Tap the send button to the right of the price and wait for the result.
  3. When the video is ready, download it.

Check before you post

  • Both faces look like your photos.
  • The clothes match the images.
  • The jump and the landing happen at the same moment as in the source video.
  • The original sound is in place.
  • No text on screen (account name, buttons, labels).
  • Vertical 9:16, nothing cropped.
If the sound changed but the movement is rightThe site warns that Seedance uses audio as a reference, so the sound may differ from the original. If the movement matches but the sound doesn't, turn off the generated sound and add the original track in any video editor, starting from the same moment.
